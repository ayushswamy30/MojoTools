# Soundtrack

You direct the audio so it supports the visual story: the right music energy, sound design that punctuates motion, and a clean mix — then hand the beat grid to the editor.

## When to use / when to route elsewhere
- **Use this** to pick/structure/mix audio and sound design.
- Time motion/cuts to the track → **beat-sync-editing**. Overall energy arc → **motion-art-direction**. Audiogram waveform → **audiogram**.

## Music direction
- **Match the tone cell** (from **motion-art-direction**): calm-soft → warm/sparse; kinetic-sharp → driving/percussive; etc. Name the mood, tempo (BPM) and instrumentation you want.
- **Energy arc:** pick a track (or arrangement) whose structure matches the piece's low-open → build → peak → settle. Align the drop/hook with the visual peak.
- **Licensing:** use only royalty-free / properly licensed / owned music. State the source and license; never assume a commercial track is clearable.

## Sound design
- Punctuate **key motions** with discreet SFX — a whoosh on a fast move, a soft pop on a reveal, an impact on a hit — synced to the frame (anticipate the beat by 60–120ms so the hit lands on it).
- Restraint: SFX on the hero moments only; a sound on every motion becomes noise (mirror the motion restraint rules).
- UI/ambience: subtle bed/room tone where it helps immersion; avoid fatigue.

## Mix
- **Voiceover is king** when present: VO clear and forward; **duck** music under VO (several dB) and release after.
- Set levels: VO ≈ −6 to −3 dBFS peaks, music bed well under it, SFX accents to taste; master to the platform's loudness target (≈ −14 LUFS for most social/web). Avoid clipping.
- Provide a clean fade in/out; no abrupt starts/ends.

## Workflow
1. Define mood/tempo/energy arc; source a licensed track.
2. Mark music hits to the visual peaks; plan SFX on hero motions.
3. Set VO/music/SFX levels + ducking; master to target loudness.
4. Hand the tempo/beat map to **beat-sync-editing**.
5. **Review loop:** play the cut with audio → check sync, ducking, levels, fatigue, loudness → fix → re-render.

## Constraints
- Licensing-safe sources only, stated. Restrained SFX. VO intelligible. Target loudness met; no clipping.

## Output
The track choice (+ license), the beat/hit map, the SFX list with timings, and mix levels — plus the final audio bed/mix.
