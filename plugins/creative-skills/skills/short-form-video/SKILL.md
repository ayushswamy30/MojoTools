# Short-Form Video

You plan 9:16 video built for the feed: a hook that stops the scroll in under 3 seconds, relentless pacing, and captions that carry the whole thing sound-off.

## When to use / when to route elsewhere
- **Use this** for vertical social shorts (typically 7–45s).
- Build/render → **remotion-video**. Longer teaching → **explainer-video**. Paid conversion ads → **ad-creative-video**. Music/beat → **soundtrack** + **beat-sync-editing**. Caption bars → **lower-thirds**.

## Rules of the format
- **9:16 (1080×1920).** Keep text and faces within the central safe zone — platform UI covers the top ~12% and bottom ~20%.
- **Hook in <3s:** open on the most arresting frame/claim/visual. State the payoff or tension immediately; no logo intros, no slow builds.
- **Retention pacing:** change something every 1–3s (cut, zoom, text pop, b-roll). Keep forward momentum; cut dead air.
- **Captions are mandatory and styled:** large, legible, animated word-by-word (karaoke/pop), high contrast with a stroke/scrim. Most viewers watch muted.
- **Sound:** ride a track/beat (**beat-sync-editing**); sync visual hits to it.
- **Loop / payoff:** end on a beat that either loops seamlessly or delivers a clear payoff + soft CTA.

## Workflow
1. Pick the **one hook** and the payoff; write them first.
2. Script tight captions (spoken cadence, punchy).
3. Beat-by-beat storyboard at 1–3s granularity, each with a visual change; mark the hook frame and the loop/payoff.
4. Style captions + choose track; sync to beats.
5. **Render loop:** rough cut → watch muted on a phone-sized frame → kill any slow moment, verify hook lands in <3s, captions legible in safe zone → refine.

## Constraints
- Safe zones respected; captions always legible muted; hook never buried; no wasted seconds.

## Quality bar
Stops the scroll in 3s, holds to the end, reads fully with sound off, and either loops or pays off.

## Output
Hook + payoff, a 1–3s beat storyboard with caption text and visual changes, and the track/sync plan — ready for **remotion-video**.
