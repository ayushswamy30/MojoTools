## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name (suggestion):** **"Mojo Tools is Online"**, with the B2B tagline *"Every brand. Every tool. One quote away."*
- **Summary:** tell existing customers and nearby trade buyers that Mojo Tools now has a website where they can see the brands it stocks and get a quote in minutes, and turn that visibility into enquiries before the online shop (R1) launches.
- **Primary objective (SMART, 🟡 targets to confirm):** get **150 qualified enquiries** (quote requests + WhatsApp chats + calls from the site) within **8 weeks of launch**, with at least **60 %** from B2B buyers.
- **Secondary objectives:**
  - Site indexed and ranking on page 1 for "Mojo Tools" and for "[brand] dealer in [city]" for the top 10 brands, within 8 weeks.
  - Google Business Profile verified, with **25+ reviews** by L+8.
  - **First online orders:** target set with the owner before launch (🟡).

## 2. Target audience

**Primary: B2B trade buyers (consideration → decision).**
> Contractors, workshop owners, factory maintenance/procurement staff and resellers within delivery range, who need genuine branded tools at trade prices and want quick answers on price and stock. They find suppliers through **Google search and Maps, WhatsApp, IndiaMART, word of mouth and brand dealer lists**, and care most about **genuine stock, price, availability and a GST invoice**.

**Secondary: dealers / resellers** who could buy brands Mojo distributes (want price lists and dealer terms).

**Tertiary: B2C walk-in / DIY buyers** (awareness). Mobile-first, found via Google Maps and Instagram/YouTube, want offers and to know the shop is nearby and trusted.

**Pain points to speak to:**

| Pain | Message angle |
|------|---------------|
| "Is it genuine?" (fakes are common in tools) | Authorised distributor; brand logos; GST invoice |
| "I have to call five shops for a price" | One quote form or WhatsApp, reply within 1 working day ✅ |
| "Is it in stock?" | Brand range shown; ask on WhatsApp for live stock |
| "I need it billed properly" | GST invoice, GSTIN on bill |
| Dealers: "What's my margin?" | Request dealer price list |

## 4. Channel strategy

The audience is mostly local and repeat B2B, so **owned and local channels come first and paid comes last**.

| # | Channel | Type | Why it fits | Formats | Effort |
|---|---------|------|-------------|---------|--------|
| 1 | **Existing customer base** (WhatsApp broadcast lists, SMS, in-store) | Owned | Fastest and cheapest enquiries; these buyers already trust Mojo | Owner message, QR standees at counter, QR on invoices, bills and visiting cards, staff mention at billing | Low |
| 2 | **Google Business Profile** | Owned / earned | Most local "tool shop near me" and "[brand] dealer" searches end here | Verified profile, categories, photos, products (brand showcase), weekly posts, review requests | Low–Medium |
| 3 | **Website SEO** (on-page + local) | Owned | Brand pages are natural landing pages for "[brand] dealer [city]" | `/brands/[brand]` pages with real copy, schema.org Organization/LocalBusiness, sitemap, Search Console | Medium |
| 4 | **WhatsApp Business** | Owned | Indian trade buyers already order this way | Business profile, catalogue of brands/categories, quick replies, click-to-chat on the site with prefilled message + page source | Low |
| 5 | **Brand partners** | Earned | Brands list authorised dealers on their own sites | Ask each brand to add Mojo + website link to its dealer locator; co-branded launch post | Low–Medium |
| 6 | **B2B marketplaces** (IndiaMART / TradeIndia) | Earned / paid | Where procurement teams search for suppliers | Update the listing (or create one) to link to the website; consistent name, address and phone | Medium |
| 7 | **LinkedIn** (company page + owner profile) | Owned | Reaches factory procurement and institutional buyers | Launch post, brand spotlights, "how we serve factories" | Low |
| 8 | **Instagram / YouTube Shorts** | Owned | B2C reach and social proof | Reels: unboxing new stock, tool tips, warehouse tour | Medium |
| 9 | **Google Search ads** (optional) | Paid | High intent, tightly geo-targeted | Brand + "dealer/distributor" + city keywords → brand pages; call ads | Medium |
| 10 | **Meta ads** (optional) | Paid | Retarget site visitors; local awareness | Click-to-WhatsApp ads, geo-radius | Medium |

**Sequencing rule:** paid ads only start **after** Search Console, GA4 conversion events and the enquiry flow have been checked working, from L+2 at the earliest.

## 7. Success metrics

| KPI | Target 🟡 | Tracked by |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| **Qualified enquiries** (primary) | 150 in 8 weeks, ≥60 % B2B | `enquiries` table + GA4 `generate_lead` + WhatsApp/call click events |
| Enquiry → quote sent → order | Track the baseline; aim for 25 % enquiry-to-order | Sales updates `enquiries.status` (manual in R0) |
| Enquiries by source | Know the top 3 sources by L+4 | UTM + `source_page` stored on each enquiry |
| GBP reviews / rating | 25+ reviews, ≥4.5 | GBP insights |
| Organic search | Indexed by L+1; page 1 for brand name + 5 "[brand] dealer [city]" terms | Search Console |
| Business account registrations | Track baseline | `companies` table |
| Ad cost per enquiry (if paid) | ≤ ₹300 🟡 | Google Ads / Meta + GA4 |

**Tracking requirements for the build** (specified in [`ARCHITECTURE.md §11`](ARCHITECTURE.md#11-analytics--attribution)):

| Event (GA4) | Fires when | Parameters |
|-------------|-----------|------------|
| `generate_lead` | Enquiry/quote form submitted successfully | `enquiry_type`, `brand`, `category`, `source_page` |
| `whatsapp_click` | Floating button or any WhatsApp CTA clicked | `placement`, `page` |
| `call_click` | `tel:` link clicked | `placement`, `page` |
| `price_list_request` | "Request price list" chosen | `brand` |
| `brand_view` | Brand page viewed | `brand` |
| `notify_signup` | Newsletter signup | `page` |

- Capture `utm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign` on first landing (first-party cookie) and save them on the enquiry row.
- Every QR code, broadcast link and social link uses a UTM URL, e.g. `?utm_source=instore&utm_medium=qr&utm_campaign=launch`.
- Pre-fill WhatsApp click-to-chat with the page name ("Hi, I'm interested in Bosch tools [from website]"), so WhatsApp leads can be attributed.
- Analytics loads only **after cookie consent** (DPDP, PRD GL-6). Enquiry counts in the database work without consent, so they are the source of truth for the primary KPI.

**Reporting cadence:** a weekly 15-minute look (enquiries by source, top pages, ad spend), the mid-point review at L+4, and the full report at L+8.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Enquiries arrive but nobody follows up fast.** Leads go cold, and buyers won't come back. | Publish the "within 1 working day" promise; email + WhatsApp alert to sales on every enquiry; one named owner per day; track `status`. |
| **Missing content and assets delay launch** (logos, photos, brand copy are all still ❓). | Start asset collection at L-4; launch with the top 10 brands complete and add others weekly. |
| **Buyers used to ordering by phone ignore the website.** | Staff point every caller to quick order / quotes; QR codes on invoices; WhatsApp stays as a fallback. |
| **Spam on the quote form** skews the numbers. | Turnstile + rate limit; count only enquiries sales marks `qualified`. |
| **Brand trademark use.** Using brand logos and names in ads or on pages without permission. | Use the "authorised" badge only where `brands.is_authorised` is true; keep authorisation letters; don't bid on brand terms where the brand forbids it. |
